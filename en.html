Orthopedic insoles

Insoles made only for your feet

Off-the-shelf insoles are made for an “average” foot. We first examine your feet and assess what you need. If insoles are needed, we make them to your measurements in the same visit. They are 2–4 mm thick and fit any shoes.

  1. ExaminationAssessing your feet and gait
  2. MeasurementsCapturing the shape of your feet
  3. MakingMade for you in the same visit
  4. FittingYou feel the result right away
